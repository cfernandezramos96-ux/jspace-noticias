Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

ElKazuoTV Uploader ("the tool") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel ElKazuoTV to upload his own videos to his own channel. The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • Through YouTube API Services, the tool accesses the authorized user's own YouTube channel: its ID and title, and the videos the user chooses to upload, together with their title, description, tags, category and privacy status.
  • It accesses no other personal information, no contacts and no data from any other YouTube user or channel.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos to his own channel and to confirm that the upload went to the correct channel. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

How the data is stored

The tool runs only on the owner's personal computer. The OAuth access token granted by Google is stored locally on that computer, so the owner does not have to sign in again for every upload. The video files and their metadata are the owner's own files on that computer. No data is sent to or stored on any server run by us.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, transfer or disclose any user data to third parties. The only data sent outside the computer is the upload itself, which goes directly to YouTube (Google) through the YouTube Data API.

Cookies

The tool does not place, access or recognize cookies or similar technologies on any device or browser.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the tool's access to your YouTube account at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the locally stored token stops working and is deleted from the owner's computer. You can also ask us to delete any data by writing to the email below; we will answer within 7 days.
